## Users

Nuclii serves both sides of a real-world experience marketplace: attendees looking for things happening near them; hosts, society leads, community organisers, service providers, talent, creatives, and venues who need structure for discovery, booking, access, and coordination; plus early contributors, partners, and investors evaluating whether the platform is credible and alive.

Visitors often arrive before the full product exists. They need to understand what Nuclii is, see that it is broader than a generic events app, and take a clear next step. Students are one early community, not the whole audience.

## Product Purpose

Nuclii is a UK-based real-world experience platform, positioned as infrastructure for real-life experiences: discovery, hosting, booking, access, and coordination in one place. The website explains the category, gets people signed up, attracts early hosts and communities, recruits contributors, collects partner and investor interest, and makes the product feel active before launch.

The primary visitor action is **signing up on the web**. Web accounts (sign up / log in) and a create flow are planned; until those pages exist, the `create`, `Log in`, and `sign up` destinations point at the early-access flow (`/join`). Explore currently points at `/events` (gated, "coming soon").

Success means visitors quickly understand that Nuclii helps people discover, host, book, and manage real-world experiences without relying on followers, group chats, or social pressure, and then sign up.

## Positioning

Not a feed and not a ticketing tool: an operating layer for real-life moments that connects every side (attendees, hosts, venues, talent, societies) with audience controls (building, uni, society, interests, eligibility such as age), privacy-first location reveal, and built-in group coordination. "If people can show up to it, it can belong on nuclii."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Launch city: Birmingham first.** Example events, places, and landmarks should lean on Birmingham. (Older copy referencing London, and the `/demo` sandbox's Lagos framing, predate this decision.)
- **Mobile app:** planned, presented as "coming soon"; store badges are display-only, not links, until release.
- **Web accounts and create flow:** planned, not yet built (see Product Purpose for current routing).
- Main navigation: explore · for hosts (→ Partner With Us, `/early-access`) · studio (→ Build With Us form) · Log in · sign up, plus the accessibility control. `demo`, `motives`, `create`, and `home` are kept in config but hidden from the nav; the `/demo` page still exists.
- Footer-only surfaces: Investors, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Cookie Policy, Community Guidelines. Never in the main navigation.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Nuclii (lowercase "nuclii" in running voice).
- **Core line: "more than memories."** This is the durable brand anchor and homepage headline, replacing "Every event starts here" as the anchor line.
- **Nuclii lime `#A3BF1C`** is the exact brand accent (logo, CTAs, motion controls). Do not approximate it.
- **Logo assets:** `public/logo/nuclii-icon.png` (mark), `public/logo/nuclii-logo-full-black.png` and `nuclii-logo-full.png` (wordmarks). Do not recolour or redraw.
- **Voice:** confident but not arrogant, warm but not childish, simple but not basic, ambitious without hype. Investor-ready and Gen Z / young professional friendly without slang overload or nightlife-only positioning. Clear, premium, calm, alive, safe, modern, trustworthy, culturally aware, product-led.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Absent, do not fabricate:** traction numbers, user counts, testimonials presented as real customers, press, partnerships, pricing, financial claims, or app-store availability.

## Anti-references

Nuclii must not look or sound like a generic events app, nightlife app, student-only app, social media feed, follower-first platform, influencer-first platform, Eventbrite clone, Instagram clone, TikTok-like feed, or party flyer site.

Avoid fake traction numbers, public financial claims, fake urgency, overpromising, long corporate paragraphs, vanity metrics, and public attendee-list framing.

## Product Principles

- **More than memories.** Nuclii is about making real-world moments happen and keep happening, from idea to audience, not just capturing them.
- **Infrastructure, not a feed.** A structured real-life operating layer, never an endless social stream or popularity contest.
- **Start close, branch out.** Discovery starts from where people live, study, and belong, then widens by interest. Map-first and spatial.
- **Privacy-first by default.** Hosts choose who sees an event; locations can reveal later; attendee visibility stays restrained.
- **Many roles, one ecosystem.** Attendees, hosts, societies, service providers, talent, venues, partners, and contributors all belong without the site becoming a directory of disconnected audiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the operating accessibility bar for public marketing pages. Preserve semantic HTML, visible focus states, labelled controls, useful alt text, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives (every auto-moving section has a pause control and a reduced-motion path).

Sign-up and early-access forms must stay short and purposeful, include 16+ confirmation where the flow collects launch interest, include marketing consent for updates, and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
