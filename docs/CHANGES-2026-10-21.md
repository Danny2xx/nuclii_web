## New pages

### `/who-its-for`: who nuclii is made for
- **Opening:** "if you have an idea, you can make it happen." Then: "No audience? No network? No venue? No need." and "the people, the talent, the venues and the tools are already here. plus it's **free**."
- **Jump links:** under the opening, links jump to each section.
- **Five audience sections,** alternating light and dark, each with the problem it solves, how nuclii helps, an example ticket, its tools and a **try on web** button:
  - **Students:** your home, your building, your campus; closed spaces with host approval; uni-email-checked student spaces; start your own community.
  - **Organisers:** reach down to the street and postcode, venues and talent in the app, co-organisers and handing over control, updates that land.
  - **Offline communities:** "the same faces, every week."
  - **Venues:** visibility, and matching with events that suit your space.
  - **Talent & makers:** just starting out or new to a scene; get on line-ups and build a portfolio.
- **Partner links:** every section except students links to the partner page.
- **Closing band:** "every account gets" (free, 18+, no follower or going counts, audience controls, private address), then "coming soon for hosts" (sponsorship, paid promotion).
- **Content:** `components/who/who-content.ts` holds one shared list of tool names.

### `/partner`: partner with us
This replaces `/early-access`, which now permanently redirects here. That also gets rid of its broken images.
- **The picker:** "let's make something together." with a **who are you?** row (societies & unis, venues & spaces, talent & makers, organisers & communities, brands (soon)). Picking a type swaps in what partnering looks like for that type.
- **Benefits:** eight, as hover cards: featured placement, priority recommendation, co-hosted events, introductions, shout-outs, founding partner badge, first access to new tools, a direct line to the team.
- **How it works:** three steps, with replies within 3–5 business days.
- **The form:** builds a live **partner pass** ticket as you fill it in. It gets a "sent" stamp on submit, and brands see "register interest".
- **Submissions** go to `app/api/partner/route.ts`.

### `/studio`: nuclii studio
The team's own corner. Black throughout, with a journal rail on the right.
- **Hero:** the looping `hero_main` video under a dark wash, with the spray-painted **nuclii studio** tag (`public/logo/nuclii-studio-spray.webp`) across its bottom edge.
- **About us:** a tilted lime ticket: "tbh, nuclii was built for us…" / "now it's yours. make the moments happen, make the memories last." It has a **tap in** button, which goes to `/join`.
- **The line up:** shows the current month, updated automatically. Draggable pinned posters: UFC tournament, unpopular opinions, puff puff contest.
- **Thinking of hosting:** sticky notes with "I'd come" (a fun reaction only; nothing is saved): pitch my friend, sunday potluck, board games night, are we compatible?
- **The memories wall:** a frameless calendar grid with photos taped to the days they happened. Clicking a photo shows the date and event, and arrows flip through the months.
- **Join the team:** "we're the ones who turn a quiet tuesday into a 6am story…", a draggable photo pile, and a **HELP WANTED** flyer whose tear-off tabs open the join-the-team pop-up.
- **Journal rail:**
  - **Trailer:** a paused clip that plays once from its corner button; tapping the clip opens TikTok.
  - **Coming up:** **spelling bee** with a "£100 voucher up for grabs" stamp and **register interest**. Setting `comingUp` to `null` shows a "catch-up" list instead.
- **Everything on the page** is edited in `components/studio/studio-content.ts`.

## Forms and endpoints

| Form | Endpoint | Sends to |
|---|---|---|
| Partner (`/partner`) | `app/api/partner/route.ts` | `PARTNER_EMAIL`, default **hello.nuclii@gmail.com** |
| Join the team (Studio pop-up) | `app/api/team/route.ts` | `TEAM_EMAIL`, default **hello.nuclii@gmail.com** |

- **Behaviour:** both forms check the answers on the server, email the team (the reply goes straight to the applicant), send the applicant a confirmation, and add them to the Resend list only if they opted in.
- **Join the team** is a two-page pop-up:
  - **Page 1, about you:** name, email, phone, city, 18+, student and uni, socials (optional), what you are, what you want to help with, and a short "a bit about you".
  - **Page 2, the small print:** four required agreements, plus optional WhatsApp and updates ticks.
  - **The button** says **join**.
  - **Under-18s** are stopped politely.
  - It replaces the Tally form.
- **Setup:** both need `RESEND_API_KEY` set in Vercel to actually send. Without it, they show an "email us" message.
