# Changes: 23–24 October 2026

Merged to `main` on `Danny2xx/nuclii_web` and live on nuclii.co.uk. Follows [CHANGES-2026-10-22.md](CHANGES-2026-10-22.md) (the speed pass).

Most of this is the mobile pass: on phones, many new visitors stopped at the phone mockup in the explore section, thinking the site ended there.

---

## Home: explore section
- **Intro line:** "your city's already moving." now sits above the phone mockup, with a bouncing "scroll" cue below it. Both fade out as soon as the cards start moving.
- **Peeking cards:** three event cards already stick out of the phone before you scroll, so it's clear there's more to see.
- **No tab bar on the mockup:** its bottom tab bar looked like the website's own nav. The feed now fades out there instead.
- **Shorter pin on phones:** the pinned stretch is 220vh, down from 280vh.
- **Screen sizes:** all of the above applies on every screen size, with a smaller peek on desktop.
- **Fix:** a dark strip no longer shows under the panel on iPhone when Safari hides its toolbar. The panel fills the large viewport and centres its content in the small one.

## Home: hero
- **New line:** "Turn any idea into an experience, fill the room with the people it's for. Then, do it again."
- **Line break:** "Then, do it again." sits on its own line on phones only.
- **Ticket buttons:** the explore and create tickets are a size smaller on phones.
- **Pause:** pausing the hero now also pauses the moving category strip below it, and that section's own toggle stays in step.

## Home: other sections
- **Concept statement:** "you make it. you book it. you go." sits on one line on phones, sized to fit.
- **Mode carousel:** can be swiped.
- **03 collaborate:** holds still. It shows the open call beside the finished poster with the line-up filled. The only motion is the stamp, now reading **"go live"** (it was "ready to publish"), which lands once as it scrolls into view.
- **04 circle chat:** trimmed to four lines, filling in from the top, with a shorter pin:
  1. Hamza's question
  2. the address reveal
  3. Maya's reply
  4. Kai's reply

## Navigation
- **Phones, over the opening section:** a smaller "sign up" ticket (no arrow) plus a "menu" button. After the opening section, just the menu button. Log in is in the menu.
- **Menu button:** square on every screen size, matching the ticket buttons. It was a circle.
- **Log in:** off the top bar on every screen size. It's in the menu only, since login and sign up both go to `/join` until real login exists. The bar is now the links (over the opening section), the sign up ticket and the menu.
- **Legal pages:** extra top space on phones, so their titles clear the bar.

## Studio page
