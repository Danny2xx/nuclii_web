# Changes: 22 October 2026

Branch `new-design`, following commit `224cda4`. Not merged to `main`.

A speed pass: the home page lagged while scrolling, and the hero videos stuttered. Measured by scrolling the whole page at a steady speed and timing every frame, before (live site) and after (production build).

---

## Nav
- **Phones:** log in and sign up no longer sit in the top bar below 768px, where they crowded the page. They're still in the menu. Tablets and desktop are unchanged.
- **Hero fold:** the "have we scrolled past the opening section" check is now an IntersectionObserver, so scrolling never forces a layout.
- **Light/dark check:** the nav's "is the page behind me light?" check hit-tests the page, which forces a full layout. It ran on every scroll frame (1.4s of a 14s profile). It now runs at most every 120ms while you scroll, and not at all on phones, where the bar is hidden.

## Explore sequence ("now make your own.")
- **On the compositor:** where the browser supports scroll-driven animations (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26), each card's, the phone's, the feed's, the dark fade's and the title's whole journey is sampled once into keyframes and run by the browser's compositor on a view timeline. It tracks the scroll exactly, off the main thread, with no spring lag. `fill: both` holds the end state, so nothing snaps back after the section.
- **Fallback:** other browsers (Firefox) keep the spring-smoothed JS version, now computing each card's journey once per frame instead of five times.
- **Unchanged:** same layout and timing. Screenshots match the live site at every step.

## Concept section
- **Category strip:** it drifted by setting `scrollLeft` from JavaScript every frame. It's now a CSS transform animation (the existing `nuclii-marquee`), at the same speed. You can still swipe the strip, and hover, touch or focus still pauses it.

## Globe
- **Redraws:** capped at 30fps (the spin is slow enough that it can't be seen), and paused while the page is being scrolled. It resumes where it stopped.

## Videos
- **Hero clips** (`public/videos/videos_web2`): re-encoded to 720p at about 1.8 Mbps, with the 1.5x speed-up built in, so the browser plays them at 1x instead of decoding faster than real time. Each is trimmed to what's ever shown (6.5s), with 1-second keyframes. 35 MB down to 11 MB.
  - `matchday`, `pickup` and `runclub` are too short to speed up without looping mid-shot, so they now play at 1x.
- **Poster:** the hero shows a still (`movie-poster.jpg`) while the first clip loads, instead of black.
- **Buffering:** the next hero clip now buffers in full before its turn, so switches never wait on the network. The prewarm helper no longer forces every carousel clip to download at once (it starved the clip on screen).
- **Memories clips:** re-encoded to 540px wide (`partyb` 4.6 MB down to 1.4 MB). Each starts playing as its ticket develops, instead of all three at once.

## Results (frames over 25ms while scrolling the home page)
- **Desktop:** 11% before, 7–8% after.
  - Explore sequence: 8% to 2%.
  - Globe: 87% to 8%.
  - FAQ: 23% to 3%.
- **Phone simulation** (4x CPU slowdown): average frame 94ms before, 64ms after.
  - Explore sequence: 286ms to 63ms.
  - These numbers are inflated by software rendering in the test browser. A real phone renders on its GPU.
- **Page load:** images and video downloaded during the test went from about 25 MB to about 6 MB.
- **Other pages** (studio, who it's for, partner, events) were already smooth: about 1% slow frames.
- **Errors:** all pages load with no console errors or failed requests.
